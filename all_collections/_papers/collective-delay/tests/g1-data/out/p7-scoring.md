The outcomes (lead times) were known to the coder before scoring. Read this as exploratory scoring, not a pre-registration. ## Rubric
**V, effective veto players, scored 1–5.**
- 1: a single executive decision.
- 2: unified government, and the resolving vehicle needs only a simple majority (reconciliation, or no filibuster relevance).
- 3: unified government, but the filibuster or a cross-party supermajority binds; or a single parliament with a comfortable majority that still needs external ratification.
- 4: divided government (one chamber or the executive opposed); or a legislature controlled by the opposition, with the executive suspended.
- 5: divided government plus the filibuster with narrow margins; or multiple sovereign parties that must agree (7 Basin States plus federal; EU27 unanimity plus a hung Commons).

**L^R, incumbents' stake in the status quo or in delay, scored 1–5.**
- 1: none.
- 3: moderate. Bargaining leverage, or diffuse sectoral stakes.
- 5: large, concentrated and organised benefit tied to the status-quo terms (e.g. senior water rights; current-law tax rates for high earners; benefit levels of current retirees).
