Case: SAS EVAAS value-added model, Houston Independent School District.

## A+0 definition
A+0 = 2007: HISD's district-wide use of value-added data from EVAAS begins (HISD/Battelle FAQ: "used value-added data since 2007"; ASPIRE award model originally approved Jan 2006, re-adopted by the board 13 Sept 2007; payouts each January from 2008). Use in appraisal/termination: board policy change Feb 2010 (A+3); new SAS licence 2011 and appraisal implementation 2012 (A+4/A+5, per Courthouse News). Note the alternative anchor (2011/2012 licence for appraisal) if the coordinator prefers adoption-for-termination; then lawsuit = A+2/A+3, ruling = A+5/A+6.
Conversions: 2006 = A-1, 2010 = A+3, 2011 = A+4, 2012 = A+5, 2014 = A+7, 2016 = A+9, 2017 = A+10.

## Decisions and caveats
- Only two formal episodes found. The board's non-renewal (E2) is included because the task asked for HISD's later decisions on EVAAS and it is an executive act by the operator's governing body targeting the model; coordinator may judge it is not a "challenge".
- Context items: Feb 2010 board policy (adoption of use, not a challenge); July 2010 senate committee questioning (no formal inquiry/finding); 2012 academic study (informal academic criticism); annual HISD ASPIRE programme evaluations (operator's own reviews of the bonus programme, not of the model; not listed as episodes - flag).
- "Procedural due process" claim described without the banned word as the claim that teachers had no fair chance to contest; "substantive due process" described as vagueness/no-rational-link claims.
