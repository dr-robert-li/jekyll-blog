# G15 preparer notes (private; coders never see this)

Case: Idaho Department of Health and Welfare (IDHW) Adult DD budget tool (individualized budget calculation tool, inputs from Inventory of Individual Needs (IIN) and SIB-R), K.W. v. Armstrong.
Adoption anchor: A+0 = July 2011, when IDHW put into use the revised budget tool (re-estimated from 2009–2010 data; constant cut from $54,965.65 to $24,476.75; new "living situation" weights; mobility variable dropped). Earlier budget tools existed (settlement notice says tools before 2008 may not be available), but the original introduction date was not found in authoritative sources; the July 2011 version is the tool every ruling addresses. Fractions: Jan 2012 A+0.5; Mar 2012 A+0.7; Aug 2012 A+1.1; Apr 2013 A+1.75; Jun 2015 A+3.9; Mar 2016 A+4.7; Sep 2016 A+5.2; Jan 2017 A+5.5; Oct 2019 A+8.3; Dec 2020 A+9.4; Jun 2022 A+10.9; Sep 2023 A+12.2; Nov 2024 A+13.3; Mar 2025 A+13.7.
