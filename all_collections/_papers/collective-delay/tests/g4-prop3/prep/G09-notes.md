# G09 preparer notes (PRIVATE - coders never see)

Case: Australian Robodebt scheme (Online Compliance Intervention, later Employment Income Confirmation / Check and Update Past Income). A+0 = July 2016 (OCI launch; mass debt raising from Sept 2016).

## Token key
- [MODEL] = Online Compliance Intervention (OCI) / "Robodebt"
- [AGENCY-1] = Department of Human Services (Centrelink), later Services Australia; policy department = Department of Social Services (DSS)
- [AGENCY-2] = Commonwealth Ombudsman
- [AGENCY-3] = Australian Federal Police
- [AGENCY-4] = National Anti-Corruption Commission (NACC); inspector = Inspector of the NACC (Gail Furness SC); reconsideration by Geoffrey Nettle AC (former High Court justice); NACC Commissioner Paul Brereton
- [AGENCY-5] = Australian Public Service Commissioner (Gordon de Brouwer)
- tax authority = Australian Taxation Office; auditor-general = ANAO
- [TRIBUNAL-1] = Administrative Appeals Tribunal (first tier = Social Services and Child Support Division "AAT1"; second tier = General Division)
- [COURT-1] = Federal Court of Australia
- [COMMITTEE-1] = Senate Community Affairs References Committee
- [INQUIRY-1] = Royal Commission into the Robodebt Scheme (Commissioner Catherine Holmes AC SC, former Chief Justice of Queensland)
- [PLAINTIFF-1] = Madeleine Masterton (Masterton v Secretary DHS, VID73/2019)
- [PLAINTIFF-2] = Deanna Amato (Amato v Commonwealth, VID611/2019)
- [PLAINTIFF-3] = Katherine Prygodicz (Prygodicz v Commonwealth, VID1252/2019); E14 appellants Nathan Knox and David Mandell (Knox v Commonwealth)

- [ORG-2] = Gordon Legal

- AAT1 decisions 2016-2022 (Kyrou J: 431 questioning averaging, ~114 accepting); figures elsewhere 210/220/424
