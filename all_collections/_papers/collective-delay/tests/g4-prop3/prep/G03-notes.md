# G03 preparer notes (PRIVATE - coders never see this)

Case: Structured-finance (RMBS/CDO) rating models of Moody's, S&P and Fitch before 2008.

## A+0 definition
A+0 = 2004. Reasoning: there is no single adoption event. By 2004 the agencies' quantitative structured-finance models (S&P LEVELS for RMBS and CDO Evaluator for CDOs; Moody's RMBS models and binomial-expansion/CDOROM approach; Fitch ResiLogic/VECTOR) were in routine use, ratings by NRSROs were embedded in US regulation (SEC net capital rule, Rule 2a-7, bank capital rules) and Basel II (published June 2004) wrote external ratings into bank capital; and the DOJ/state settlements' statements of facts cover conduct from 2004 (S&P 2004-2007; Moody's 2004-2010). Alternative anchors: 1996 (LEVELS/BET models) or 2001 (S&P CDO Evaluator). If coordinator re-anchors, shift all offsets uniformly.
Conversions: 2006 = A+2, 2007 = A+3, 2008 = A+4, 2009 = A+5, 2010 = A+6, 2011 = A+7, 2012 = A+8, 2013 = A+9, 2014 = A+10, 2015 = A+11, 2016 = A+12, 2017 = A+13, 2018 = A+14, 2025 = A+21.
