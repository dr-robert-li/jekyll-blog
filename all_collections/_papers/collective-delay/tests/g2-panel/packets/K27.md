# Evidence packet K27
Label: Chemical family X, chlorinated aromatic industrial fluids and waxes used mainly as insulating and heat-resistant materials in electrical equipment
De-identification: partly de-identified (substance and company names replaced); coders may recognise the case
t0 (first credible early warning): 1937
Scoring point: t0 + 5 = 1942. Every item below is dated at or before this year.

Note on t0: EEA's synthesis chapter dates the first chloracne cases to 1899. The EEA case chapter, however, attributes the 1899 cases to the chlorinated organic industry in general, 30 years before commercial production of [X] began in 1929. The first EEA-dated warning about [X] itself is the 1936–1937 investigation (EEA case-chapter table: "both labour regulators and manufacturers were made aware"), so t0 = 1937 here. The outcomes file records alternative t0 values.

Names replaced: [X] = the chemical family; [Producer M] = the sole US manufacturer from 1935; [Firm H] = a New York company using [X] together with related chlorinated compounds; [Researcher D] = a Harvard University researcher; [Electrical firm G] = a large electrical-equipment manufacturer. "EEA case chapter" = EEA (2001) *Late lessons from early warnings* I, case chapter on [X]. "Legal filing" = a 2023 US state civil complaint that describes 1930s company records; it is used only for facts dated 1935–1939.
